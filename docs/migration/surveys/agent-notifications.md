## What the daemon-side landing does and does not give you

The daemon can now hold an enrolment and deliver an encrypted notification to it. Two halves are still
missing, and both are named here so the next unit does not rediscover them:

- **The browser cannot receive one.** `packages/pwa` has no service worker at all — no registration, no
  `push` listener, no `showNotification`. `pushManager.subscribe` needs a registration, so in a real
  browser enrolment cannot even begin. Every daemon-side guarantee is still real (an endpoint is
  validated against its push service, not against whether a tab drew a toast), but push is not
  end-to-end until a worker lands.
- **Nothing raises a notification.** `PushService.notify` fans out to every device that agreed, prunes
  endpoints a push service reports as gone, and is called in production by exactly one thing: the
  confirmation sent when a device enrols. The presenter that decides WHEN to speak — a durable attention
  item, a session that failed, the agent-callable direct notification and its audit journal — is the work
  described in the table above, and the prohibition in the previous paragraph still stands.
- **`interactiveOnly` has no source.** The preference is stored, honoured and tested; the only caller
  today sends a payload about no session, for which it does not apply. A presenter that raises a
  session notification has to pass whether that session is interactive.
