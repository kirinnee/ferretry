## Asks: the agenda asks, the card shows

- Only an ask asks. When you need the owner to decide or do something, raise an ask (`fy agenda add`); a card
  (&F412) never asks, has no answer of its own, and is named on an ask at most once ("For &F412 Badge colour").
- One ask, one answer row: say what you need once, in its "What I need from you", with its own answers or words.
- The owner's answer may move linked cards (Done closes a tried card, Not good sends it back with their words). That
  is the daemon's bookkeeping: you are told the outcome; you never move a card to stand in for an answer.
- Ask only when there is something to ask, and on the topic's existing ask; never open a new ask per stage.
- Only the owner answers an ask. You never answer your own ask, and a value the owner types into an input form goes
  to its target, never to you.
