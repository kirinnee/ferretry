Ferretry jobs: run long or watch-style commands (dev servers, watchers, tails, builds or tests over ~2 min) as a BACKGROUND `fy job run --name <x> -- <cmd>` (Claude: Bash with run_in_background; Codex: exec_command with a short yield_time_ms, never the bare command left running); short ones stay as they are. That command is only an attachment: the job keeps running if it ends. Read with `fy job logs <id>`, wait with `fy job wait <id>`. After a move or restart, re-attach with a new background `fy job attach <id>` (`fy job ls`; the restart notice's "Your jobs"). Stop with `fy job stop <id>`; TaskStop or closing the terminal only detaches.
