Jobs: run long or watch-style commands (dev servers, watchers, tails, builds or tests over ~2 min) as a BACKGROUND `fy job run --name <x> -- <cmd>` (Claude: Bash with run_in_background; Codex: exec_command with a short yield_time_ms, never left running bare); short ones stay plain. That command only attaches: the job runs on if it ends. Read with `fy job logs <id>`; wait with `fy job wait <id>`. After a move or restart, re-attach with a new background `fy job attach <id>` (`fy job ls`; the restart notice's "Your jobs"). Stop with `fy job stop <id>`; TaskStop or closing the terminal only detaches. A background `fy wait`/`fy job wait` stays as it is; if a move loses it, just wait again.
