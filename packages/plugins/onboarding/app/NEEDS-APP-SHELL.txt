Files of the onboarding app half that cannot be built or tested until the app shell (packages/app) lends them what the
workspace does not have yet. They are the old PWA's onboarding feature, copied with their non-React imports re-pointed;
React, lucide-react and the shell's primitives are NOT added here.

React components (.tsx), need react, react-dom and lucide-react:
  command-block.tsx              needs ./copy-button.tsx
  copy-button.tsx
  onboarding-brand.tsx           imports ../../shell/brand-mark.tsx           (the shell's BrandMark)
  onboarding-chooser.tsx
  onboarding-connection-note.tsx
  onboarding-doer-chooser.tsx
  onboarding-page.tsx            imports ../../hooks/use-keyboard-open.ts      (the shell's useKeyboardOpen)
  onboarding-stages.tsx
  onboarding-target-chooser.tsx
  onboarding-track.tsx
  setup-diagram.tsx
  setup-handoff-panel.tsx        needs ./setup-qr.ts

Pure files that need a package or the DOM lib that the root project does not have:
  setup-qr.ts                    needs the `qrcode-generator` dependency
  landing-geometry.ts            needs `playwright-core` (type) and the DOM lib; it is a browser-harness helper

Tests that need the shell's DOM support (happy-dom, mount/interact/must from packages/app/tests/support/dom.ts):
  tests/unit/app/onboarding-connection-note.test.tsx
  tests/unit/app/onboarding-page.test.tsx
  tests/unit/app/onboarding-stages.test.tsx
  tests/unit/app/onboarding-track.test.tsx
  tests/unit/app/setup-diagram.test.tsx
  tests/unit/app/get-started-script.test.ts     (boots the get-started page's own script)
  tests/unit/app/setup-qr.test.ts               (needs qrcode-generator)

Also for the shell (not this package):
  app/get-started/index.html is served as /get-started/ by the shell's build. The shell's vite config has no entry for
  it today and its `public/_headers` CSP does not pin its one inline script. The two assertions that checked that
  (the vite entry and the CSP hash) were dropped from get-started-document.test.ts; they belong to the shell's tests.
  app/landing-geometry.ts is used by the browser harness that measures the static pages.

Everything else in app/ (onboarding-model, onboarding-progress, first-run-entry, setup-handoff, command-syntax,
connection-disclosure, pending-connections, device-kind, link-costs, onboarding-screen, index) is pure and tested without any of the above.
