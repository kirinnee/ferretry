02 Phone + every machine

Reach it from anywhere.

Run fy pair, scan the code, and your phone is in. The end-to-end encrypted relay works behind NAT with nothing to configure; the rendezvous cannot read a session body or device token. Pair every computer you run and the app lists all their sessions together, with what needs you on top.

03 Approval cards

Say yes from your pocket.

When Claude Code or Codex asks permission — to read a file outside its folder, or run a command — the prompt becomes a card in the app: allow once, always allow, or deny with a note. Nothing is answered for you.

04 The Warden

Something watches the fleet.

The Warden's rules spot sessions that are stuck, looping, sitting on an unanswered question or a dead pane — for free. Turn on review and an investigator reads the session and decides: leave it, nudge it, or bring it to you. A Jev key adds a fast guard that skips the healthy flags, and a daily cap stops surprise spend.

05 Voice

Talk instead of type.

Dictate to any session. Parakeet turns speech into text on your device, in the browser, so your audio never leaves it. If you want, Groq or Cerebras tidies the words before you send.

On-device · audio stays here

06 Lessons

Correct it once.

When you correct a ferret, it can file the lesson. You review each one, and Apply writes it into the project's CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md — or your global instructions — after showing you the diff. Older sessions can be scanned too, with a cost estimate first.

07 Peer sends

Your agents talk to each other.

Agents can hand work across the fleet. A peer send is attributed to the server-derived sender, not a name supplied in the request, so the receiving agent knows which teammate is speaking.
