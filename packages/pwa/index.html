A phone app for starting, following and steering agent sessions on your own machines — with no Ferretry server in between.

Get started in the app →How it works

There is no Ferretry server. This page is static. Your daemon stays on your machine. Pairing hands your phone a token directly, so session content, transcripts and credentials do not pass through anything Ferretry operates.

The shape of it

A direct line from your machine to your phone.

  1. Run Ferretry’s daemon on the machine that runs your agents.
  2. Pair your phone with the daemon using its QR code or pairing link.
  3. Open the app to follow the work, respond to a question, or take control.

Ferretry is for people who want to keep their agent fleet close: on their own machines, with a clear view from their phone.
