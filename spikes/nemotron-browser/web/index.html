Streaming ASR on this device

A Ferretry spike. Pick a model, load it, then play a bundled clip or speak. Nothing is uploaded: audio stays in this tab and models come straight from Hugging Face.

1 · Model

Use Wi-Fi: the Nemotron and Parakeet models are about 0.66 GB each. Try the small baseline first, then one Nemotron, then Parakeet.

2 · Run

“Live” feeds 4 LibriSpeech clips (37 s) at real speed, the way a microphone would. “Flat-out” feeds them as fast as the device can decode. Speak streams your microphone.
